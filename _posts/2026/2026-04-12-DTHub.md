[Dr. Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}), one of the faculty participants in the ICDS Digital Twins Hub’s Faculty-in-Residence Program, and Ph.D. candidate [Hongliang Li]({{ '/members/1001_hongliang-li.html' | relative_url }}) were featured in a recent Penn State News article.
