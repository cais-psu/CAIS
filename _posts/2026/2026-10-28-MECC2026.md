Congratulations to [Hongliang Li]({{ '/members/1001_hongliang-li.html' | relative_url }}) on his paper's nomination for the [Best Student Paper Award](https://mecc2026.a2c2.org/best-student-paper-nomination/) at the 2026 Modeling, Estimation and Control Conference (MECC 2026)!

The paper, “Process-Informed Energy-Aware Batch Manufacturing Scheduling with Hierarchical Digital Twins: Experiments on a Bottling Plant Testbed,” is authored by Hongliang Li, Joseph Cuiffi, Herschel Pangborn, and Ilya Kovalenko. It was selected as one of five finalists for the award.
