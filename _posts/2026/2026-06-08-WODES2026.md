CAIS Lab Presents Three Papers on Robustness, Verification, and Resilient Control

CAIS Lab members presented three recent papers on formal methods, cyber-physical systems, and intelligent manufacturing systems.

[Jonghan Lim]({{ '/members/1003_jonghan-lim.html' | relative_url }}) presented “Logic-Based Verification of Task Allocation for LLM-Enabled Multi-Agent Manufacturing Systems,” co-authored by [Jonghan Lim]({{ '/members/1003_jonghan-lim.html' | relative_url }}), [Mostafa Tavakkoli Anbarani]({{ '/members/alumni/1002_mostafa-anbarani.html' | relative_url }}), [Rômulo Meira-Góes](https://www.eecs.psu.edu/departments/directory-detail-g.aspx?q=rzm5911){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, and [Ilya Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}).

[Ilya Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}) presented “Attack-Resilient Control of Finite-State Cyber-Physical Systems via Inspection States,” co-authored by [Mostafa Tavakkoli Anbarani]({{ '/members/alumni/1002_mostafa-anbarani.html' | relative_url }}), [Jonghan Lim]({{ '/members/1003_jonghan-lim.html' | relative_url }}), [Rômulo Meira-Góes](https://www.eecs.psu.edu/departments/directory-detail-g.aspx?q=rzm5911){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, and [Ilya Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}).

[Shih-Jie Shih](https://insr.psu.edu/students/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} presented “NeuroSymbolic Robustness Analysis for Discrete Systems with Respect to Transition Deviations,” co-authored by [Shih-Jie Shih](https://insr.psu.edu/students/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, [Jonghan Lim]({{ '/members/1003_jonghan-lim.html' | relative_url }}), [Ilya Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}), and [Rômulo Meira-Góes](https://www.eecs.psu.edu/departments/directory-detail-g.aspx?q=rzm5911){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

Congratulations to all authors on sharing this work with the research community!
