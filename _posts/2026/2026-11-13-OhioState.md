On November 13, 2026, [Dr. Ilya Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}) delivered the seminar "Toward Adaptive and Safe Manufacturing Systems with Industrial Robotics, Automation, and Control" at The Ohio State University as part of the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering's Robotics, Automation and Autonomy Seminar series.

The talk covered coordinated control of manufacturing subsystems, AI-assisted human-robot collaboration in assembly, and strategies for maintaining flexibility and performance amid power grid variability and changing customer demand. Case studies from simulations and experimental testbeds illustrated these approaches.
