On October 1, 2026, [Dr. Ilya Kovalenko]({{ '/members/0000_ilya-kovalenko.html' | relative_url }}) delivered the virtual seminar "Developing Control and Automation Approaches for Adaptive and Flexible Manufacturing Systems" as part of Argonne National Laboratory's LANS Seminar series.

His talk covered the lab's work on AI-assisted human–robot collaboration, coordinating manufacturing subsystems, and adapting production to changes in power availability and customer demand.

Read more on the [Argonne National Laboratory event page](https://www.anl.gov/event/developing-control-and-automation-approaches-for-adaptive-and-flexible-manufacturing-systems){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.
